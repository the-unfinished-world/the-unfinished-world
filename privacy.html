The Unfinished World

This page explains what we keep about you when you join the collective, and what we do with it. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We never see your password.
  • Whatever you add to your profile: what you do, your interests, a few words about yourself, and a picture if you upload one.
  • Whether you chose to appear on the People page.

How we use it

To know who is in the collective and to get in touch with members about its activities. We do not sell or share your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

If you tick “Show me on the People page”, your name, picture, what you do, interests and the few words you wrote are public on this site. Your email address is never shown. If you leave the box unticked, only the people who run the collective can see your details.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored with Supabase, a database service, in the United States. Sign-in is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Walking the gorge

When you take the walker with the arrow keys, where it is, what it is doing and which character it is are sent live to other people on the site, so they can see you walk and build. This carries no name, email or account, is not stored, and stops when you stop playing. The bridges people build are saved for everyone, with no record of who built them.

Changing or deleting it

You can change your profile, or hide it from the People page, any time by signing in and opening “Your profile”. To have everything deleted, email nikhilmarudai00@gmail.com and we will remove it.
