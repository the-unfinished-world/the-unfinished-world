# Reference study — what to take from each (2026-11-07)

All files from Wikimedia Commons (public domain works / CC photos), fetched at 1600px.

## Figure on a path
- **shen-zhou-poet-mountaintop.jpg** (Shen Zhou, Ming). THE brief. Small figure with staff on a cliff lip, open sky
  above, cloud band below. Rock = light grey wash, darker graded wash along the top contour, vertical/curled texture
  strokes (cun), moss dots along ridges, black pine clusters. Clouds are empty paper with thin wobbly outline strokes.
  Pure black is rare: pines, dots, figure, a few contour accents. Everything else is 10–45% grey.
- **ma-yuan-mountain-path.jpg** (Ma Yuan, Song). One-corner composition: all mass bottom-left, two thirds empty.
  Robe drawn with few flowing contour lines; path tapers to nothing.

## Mountains / "cotton" masses
- **guo-xi-early-spring.jpg** (Guo Xi, 1072). Rounded "cloud-head" rock masses (juanyun cun = curled-cloud texture
  strokes). Dark wash on the outer edges, light centres. Mist dissolves the base of every mass so they float.
  Vertical stacking near → middle → far with decreasing contrast.
- **fan-kuan-travelers.jpg** (Fan Kuan, c.1000). Monumental cliff face, raindrop texture, tiny travellers at the
  bottom: scale = grandeur.
- **mi-youren-cloudy-mountains.jpg** / **sesshu-haboku.jpg**: wet, soft, dotted washes; splashed ink; mist as the
  main subject.
- **huangshan-cloud-sea-1/2.jpg**: cloud sea with peaks poking through; cool atmospheric gradient with distance.

## Bridges
- **hiroshige-ohashi-bridge.jpg**: wooden deck with transverse planks, railing = posts + top rail + mid rail,
  trestle piers with X-bracing under the deck, gentle arch, small hatted walkers.
- **hasui-bridge.jpg**: evening atmosphere, structure in silhouette against a graded sky.

## Rules distilled for the renderer
1. Rock masses: wash fill (gradient, darker at the top contour), brush contour with variable width, 60–150 short
   texture strokes clipped to the mass, moss dots on ridges, 1–3 pines per ledge. Cache each mass once.
2. Mist bands cut across masses: paper-coloured soft bands with thin outline strokes. Bases of masses dissolve.
3. Three depth layers with falling contrast: far peaks (flat 8–12% wash), middle masses (20–35% with contour),
   near gorge walls and ledges (full treatment).
4. Black only for: figure, pines, moss dots, contour accents, bridge timber.
5. Bridge = planks across + posts + rails + trestles; every element is a particle.
6. Composition: leave the top-left two thirds empty for the headline; gorge on the right; figure ~12% of height.
