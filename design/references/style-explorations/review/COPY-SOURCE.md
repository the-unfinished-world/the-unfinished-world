# Source: "The Unfinished World" brand doc (Google Doc), verbatim essentials

Tagline: A collective of interdisciplinary thinkers and builders questioning the boundaries of what is possible.
Mission: Bring together the deeply curious thinkers and builders across disciplines to further our collective understanding of the world and advance ambitious ideas with substantial public benefit.
Founding thesis: Some of humanity's most consequential problems offer no clear path to financial return. Their benefits may be widely shared, their timelines uncertain, and their solutions difficult to fit within existing institutions. As a result, important possibilities can remain underexplored. Commercial incentives support extraordinary innovation, but they shape which futures receive attention and resources. There is room for an organization that begins with a different question: What would be worth understanding or creating because of what it could make possible for humanity? We believe breakthroughs can emerge when different ways of understanding the world meet. A question that appears intractable within one discipline may look different through the methods, discoveries, or imagination of another. The Unfinished World brings people together to pursue that work. It creates space for rigorous discussion, unconventional questions, and collaboration across fields, and supports the development of promising ideas into research, experiments, and practical projects. AI and other emerging tools may make parts of this work more accessible... Turning that potential into meaningful progress requires expertise, judgment, collaboration, and sustained inquiry. We seek to expand both our understanding of reality and our capacity to improve life within it.
Vision: A world in which our collective knowledge and imagination help humanity pursue possibilities whose value exceeds their immediate financial return.
Community: people learn generously from one another; questions are welcome before they are well formed; disagreement deepens understanding; connections lead to collaboration.
Who: curiosity, intellectual humility, depth and generosity, imagination, initiative, public purpose. Members contribute as teachers and as learners.
How we work: 1 Learn Together (reading groups; member-led sessions). 2 Connect and Develop Ideas (frontier technology discussions; question workshops). 3 Make and Test (collective work days; writing and synthesis; experiments and projects).
