The Unfinished World
Join the Collective

A collective of interdisciplinary thinkers and builders questioning the boundaries of what is possible.

Some of humanity’s most consequential problems offer no clear path to financial return. Their benefits are widely shared, their timelines uncertain, their solutions a poor fit for existing institutions. So they stay underexplored.

Commercial incentives drive extraordinary innovation, and they also decide which futures get attention. We begin with a different question: what would be worth understanding or creating because of what it could make possible for humanity?

We are at the start of a new era. Artificial intelligence has made possible a renaissance of human-led creativity and prosperity, with fronts for curiosity that we cannot yet properly imagine. We want to stand at the frontier of what is possible and push it forward: not only building what is useful now, but reshaping how we think about reality and what could be.

Breakthroughs emerge when different ways of understanding the world meet. A question that looks intractable within one discipline may look different through the methods, discoveries, or imagination of another.

So we bring people together across fields. We read together and run member-led sessions. We workshop questions and talk through frontier technology. We spend work days making things: writing, experiments, projects. Promising ideas get help on their way to research and practice.

We look for curiosity, intellectual humility, depth, generosity, imagination, and public purpose. Questions are welcome before they are well formed. Disagreement deepens understanding. Everyone teaches and everyone learns.

We want a world in which our collective knowledge and imagination help humanity pursue possibilities whose value exceeds their immediate financial return. If that sounds like you, join the collective.
